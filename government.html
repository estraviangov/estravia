Constitutional Structure

State Governance & Branches

The constitution strictly codifies the separation of powers into three distinct branches to ensure accountability, civic representation, and law.

Executive Office

Headed by the President (Office of President Ilyomaria). Responsible for national defense, foreign diplomacy, state policy execution, and signing legislative enactments.

Bicameral Congress

Composed of the **Senate** and the **House of Representatives**. Responsible for enacting laws, fiscal budgeting, and democratic policy debates.

Civil Charter

Guarantees total separation of church and state, equality under law, protection of freedom of expression, and transparent democratic elections.
